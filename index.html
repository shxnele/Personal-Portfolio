Case study

SJC CARE

Overview Problem Users My role Process Designs Result Lessons

Project overview

SJC Care: A System for Managing JHS Guidance Records and Counseling Services at SJC-O Inc. is designed to support the Guidance Center and authorized school personnel responsible for managing student counseling records.

The problem

The process of managing student counseling records at SJC-O Inc. is currently manual and paper-based, which can lead to inefficiencies, errors, and difficulties in accessing and sharing information. The school needs a digital solution that streamlines the management of these records while ensuring data security and privacy.

Target users

  • Guidance Counselors who are responsible for recording and managing counseling data.
  • School Administration that relies on accurate and secure counseling records to support high-level decision-making.
  • SJC-O Inc. Junior High Students as the end-users of the counseling services.

My role

I worked as Design & Architecture Lead, responsible for designing the system architecture, creating the database schema, and ensuring that the application meets the needs of the target users. I also assist in Testing and Quality Assurance to ensure that the system is reliable, secure, and user-friendly.

Process

  1. Understand the problem. I reviewed the paper-based process for managing student counseling records and the challenges it creates for staff.
  2. Identify user needs. I considered the needs of guidance counselors, school administrators, and Junior High students who use the counseling services.
  3. Plan the architecture. I designed the system structure around managing guidance records and counseling services for the school.
  4. Polish the documents. I created the documentation to support the system's design, including it's mock ups.
  5. Support quality assurance. I assisted with testing and reviewed reliability, security, and usability against the system's requirements.

Designs

This conceptual workflow shows how SJC Care supports counseling services and the management of guidance records:

StudentsReceive counseling services
Guidance counselorsRecord and manage information
SJC CareOrganizes guidance records
Authorized personnelAccess records for school needs

The system architecture and database schema are designed to keep guidance information organized and support reliable, secure access for authorized school personnel.

Project outcome

The project produced a system design for managing Junior High guidance records and counseling services at SJC-O Inc. As Design & Architecture Lead, I developed the system architecture and database schema, and supported quality assurance. The design aims to help guidance counselors organize records and give authorized school personnel reliable access while prioritizing security and usability.

Lessons learned

  • Start with the real workflow. Understanding the paper-based process helps shape a digital system around the school's needs.
  • Design for the people who use it. Guidance counselors, school administrators, and students have different roles and needs.
  • Plan the data structure carefully. A thoughtful database schema helps keep guidance records organized and useful.
  • Build privacy into the design. Counseling records are sensitive, so security and authorized access need to be considered from the start.
  • Test for quality. Reviewing reliability, security, and usability helps ensure the system meets its requirements.
  • Next steps: continue validating the design with its intended users and refine the architecture and database schema as requirements become clearer.
